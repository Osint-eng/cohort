Cohort

AI workspace for student teams — owners, deadlines, and a fair record

Problem Statement

Group projects start as chaos

A WhatsApp dump. A half-empty Google Doc. Three people who each think someone else owns the slides. By week three the work is uneven, the deadline is close, and nobody can show the professor who actually did what.
  • No clear owners or due dates
  • Contribution is invisible and disputed
  • The “herder” burns out while others free-ride

REALITY CHECK

The world does not need another AI demo.

Students need something that turns the chaos into owners, deadlines, and a fair record — and that they would open again tomorrow.

Solution Overview

Cohort runs the project, not the homework

Paste a brief, chat export, or meeting notes. Cohort extracts tasks, proposes owners and due dates, and keeps a contribution log your team — and your professor — can trust.
  • Task board becomes the source of truth
  • Agent nags only what is late or blocked
  • Every accept, complete, and unblock is logged

NOT A DEMO · NOT A WRAPPER

It does not do the assignment.

It answers: Who owns what? What is blocked? What is due next?

Built as multi-player software people can actually use.

Target Users

Built for three people in the room

Student teams
Group assignments, labs, hackathons, club work — anyone who has to ship together under a deadline.
The herder
The one person who always ends up chasing everyone else. Cohort takes the admin load off their shoulders.
Professors
Want a clean contribution trail instead of a he-said-she-said email chain at grading time.

AI Technologies Used

The model is a tool, not the product

Extraction
Structured task generation
Turns free-text briefs and chat logs into titles, owners, due dates, and dependencies — not free-form prose.
Assignment
Owner & deadline proposals
Suggests who should own what based on context and past contribution signals, then lets humans confirm.
Agent
Selective nags & drafts
Surfaces only late or blocked items and drafts the check-in message so the herder doesn’t have to.
Log
Contribution trail
Records accepts, completions, and unblocks as first-class events — audit-ready for the team and professor.
Models via API. The durable value is the workflow, the live board, and the trusted log — software people would open again tomorrow.
